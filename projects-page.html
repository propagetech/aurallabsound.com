Our Sound Design Work

Aural Lab Sound has completed over 200+ projects spanning broadcast, film, web content, and interactive media. Our portfolio demonstrates expertise in synchronized sound capture, complex sound design, and high-fidelity field recording across multiple production environments. From intimate narrative dialogue recording to immersive multi-track ambience sessions, we deliver production-grade audio that meets international broadcast and streaming standards.

Our production services include feature-length film sound design, documentary field recording for international broadcasters, advertising sync sound for national campaigns, and spatial audio for VR and game development. We specialize in challenging acoustic environments—working in Bangalore's dense urban settings, monsoon weather conditions, and remote locations. Every project utilizes our primary recording chain: Sennheiser MKH precision microphones with Sound Devices MixPre 10 multi-track recording, ensuring pristine technical quality at capture.

Selected project types showcase our range: automotive sound design for luxury vehicles, architectural ambience libraries for cinematic productions, foley sessions for animated features, contact microphone recording for documentary intimacy, and live event capture with wireless transmission. Our Avid S1 Pro Tools Ultimate workflow enables seamless integration with editorial teams and post-production facilities globally.
