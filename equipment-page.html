Equipment Overview

Aural Lab Sound operates with broadcast-standard microphones and portable recording systems selected specifically for sync sound recording, foley production, and sound design applications. Our microphone collection emphasizes Sennheiser's precision engineering—renowned for reliability, low distortion, and neutral frequency response in field conditions. This equipment lineup enables simultaneous multi-microphone configurations, wireless transmission systems, and portable capture quality approaching studio-grade specifications at remote locations across Bangalore and surrounding regions.

The core recording chain centers on Sound Devices MixPre 10 multi-track recorders, professional-grade portable mixers with pristine preamp design and redundant recording capability. This pairing with Sennheiser microphones and Lectrosonics wireless systems enables production crews to capture high-fidelity dialogue, ambience, and effects simultaneously, with on-location mixing and monitoring capabilities. All recording systems feature professional XLR connectivity, phantom power stability, and built-in safety recording (backup audio tracks) standard on all sessions.

Post-production workflow integrates Avid S1 control surface with Pro Tools Ultimate, enabling professional mixing, sound design refinement, and final delivery to broadcast-standard specifications. This combination provides compatibility with international post-production facilities and supports complex multi-track editing, automation, and spatial audio delivery. Equipment available for rental to independent professionals, production companies, and crew requiring professional-grade recording systems for location work.
