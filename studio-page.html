Studio Overview

Aural Lab Sound is a specialized sound production studio located in Bangalore, India, serving documentary filmmakers, commercial production companies, game developers, and broadcast networks. Our facility combines professional-grade recording environments with post-production mixing capabilities, enabling end-to-end sound creation from location capture through final delivery. The studio operates as both a recording facility and equipment rental service, accommodating independent sound professionals and production companies requiring broadcast-standard audio services.

The studio occupies a noise-controlled environment optimized for multi-track recording, wireless transmission reliability, and field equipment staging. Our primary production suite features pro-grade monitoring calibrated to broadcast standards, with isolation treatment supporting simultaneous multi-microphone configurations. We maintain redundant recording systems, backup power infrastructure, and environmental monitoring to ensure production continuity. The facility accommodates equipment checkout for location work, comprehensive project sound supervision, and direct delivery to Pro Tools-compatible post-production pipelines.

Operating hours and booking are flexible to accommodate production schedules. We support single-day location gigs, extended field recording sessions, foley production stages, and mixing projects. Aural Lab Sound is based in Bangalore, serving clients across South India and collaborating with remote post-production partners nationally and internationally.
